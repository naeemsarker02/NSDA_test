Plant Care Questions

Repot when the roots become crowded or start growing through the drainage holes.

Choose a pot slightly larger than the current root system rather than a very large pot.

Plant Parent Tips

Watering

Check the soil before watering.

Leaves

Clean the leaves regularly.

Support

Use a moss pole as the plant grows.
